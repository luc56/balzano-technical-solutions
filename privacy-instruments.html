We do not collect any personally identifiable information from our users. The app is designed to function as a technical calculation toolkit and does not require a user account, registration, or login.

2. Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using our Service, we do not ask you to provide us with any personally identifiable information. We do not have access to your contacts, photos, or any other personal files on your device.

We do not collect information on how the Service is accessed and used. No analytics or tracking tools are integrated into the application.

The application offers a currency converter tool which uses real-time exchange rate data provided by the Frankfurter API (https://www.frankfurter.app). This specific feature requires an active internet connection to fetch the latest rates, but no personal data or device identification is transmitted during these requests.

We do not employ third-party companies or individuals to facilitate our Service, provide the Service on our behalf, or perform Service-related services. All calculations are performed locally on your device.

5. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. Since we do not collect or store personal data, the risk of data breaches is minimized. However, remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
